About us

Clothes off the rack are made for an average body. Nobody is average.

Highlands Hem and Stitch started with a simple observation: plenty of students wear clothes that don't quite fit. Pants drag on the ground, shirts hang too loose, and a great thrift find is two inches too long.

Professional tailoring could fix that, but most students never consider it. It feels expensive, it feels like something for adults, and it's not obvious where to even start.

We started this business to change that. Our goal is to make alterations affordable and easy to ask for, so every student can feel comfortable and confident in what they wear without spending too much.
